Hello I am Robert McEachron!

      I am an undergraduate, finishing my BA in psychology and my minor in statistics and data analytics. I currently work as a writing tutor at the Writing and Multiliteracy Center as well as a statistics tutor at the Learning Resource Center both located at California State University Channel Islands.
      My research interests broadly include neuroscience, cognition, and virtual reality. I am specifically curious about testing spatial cognition in virtual environments, as well as the effects of presence, and the proteus effect on behavior within simulations. I am currently conducting research examining physiological and behavioral responses to a tsunami in virtual reality.
